A Chrome extension that strips Instagram down to what you actually want — no
ads, no reels, no suggested posts or people, and a chronological feed. Inspired
by [YouTube Redux](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/youtube-redux/mdgdgieddpndgjlmeblhjgljejejkikf).
